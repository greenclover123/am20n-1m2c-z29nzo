### Game Details & Guide
My Rusty Submarine Guide



Game Overview


My Rusty Submarine is a browser-based game where you design, build, and sail your own submarine through a 3D ocean.



How to Play


To play the game, click on the 'Start' button at the top of the screen. This will begin the loading process for your submarine's vessel.



Your goal is to navigate your submarine through treacherous waters while avoiding obstacles like rocks and sea monsters.



Controls


To move your submarine, use the left and right arrow keys to steer. The 'W' key will propel your submarine forward, while the 'S' key will allow it to turn.



The submarine's speed can be adjusted using the 'Up' and 'Down' arrow keys.



Objective


Your ultimate objective is to reach the 'End of the Ocean' without touching the bottom. The game features 50 levels, each with its own unique challenges and obstacles.



Tips and Tricks for Success



Tip 1: Plan Your Route
, as certain areas of the ocean are more treacherous than others.




Tip 2: Upgrade your submarine's engines regularly to increase its speed.





Tip 3: Use your lifeboats wisely, and don't waste them on unnecessary repairs.
